Privacy Policy

Linear Algebra Visualizer

Linear Algebra Visualizer does not collect or store your personal information. We do not track you across other apps or services, and we do not build a profile of you.

To manage purchases and subscriptions, the app uses RevenueCat, a third party service. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat processes your purchase and subscription history on our behalf. This is used only to deliver the content you bought, restore your purchases, and prevent fraud. RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or contact details, and your purchase data is not used to track you.

RevenueCat is based in the United States, so this information may be processed outside the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area. You can read how RevenueCat handles data in their privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
